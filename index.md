# Privacy Policy for Wende Injili

Wende Injili is an offline hymn app designed for worshippers and choirs.  

- The app does not collect, store, or share any personal information.  
- All hymns are stored locally in a bundled SQLite database.  
- No internet connection, account login, or third-party services are required.  
- The app does not track user activity or location.  

If you have questions about this policy, please contact the developer.
